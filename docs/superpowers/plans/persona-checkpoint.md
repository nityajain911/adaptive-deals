# Persona trust and comprehension checkpoint

**Date:** 3 October 2026  
**Build under review:** `7118d98`  
**Purpose:** test whether the redesigned product explains one useful decision at a time before the submission story and product note are finalised.

This is a review worksheet, not a source of product claims. The applicant names the five reviewers or role personas and records what each person actually says. Record counts and verbatim comments; do not convert a five-person review into percentages.

## Review setup

1. Hide **Demo controls** before handing over the product.
2. Give no narration and no explanation of Adaptive Deals.
3. Ask the reviewer to think aloud, but do not teach them where to click.
4. Show only the beats assigned below, then ask the questions immediately.
5. Record the answer in the reviewer's words. Mark an answer as clear only when the reviewer can explain it without prompting.

## Six comprehension questions

Ask the same six questions without rewording them:

1. What is blocking the deal?
2. What offer is recommended?
3. What does the merchant gain and give up?
4. What should the merchant do next?
5. What is the current deal state?
6. What outcome will the product measure and learn from?

## Five persona assignments

Replace each **Name and role** field with the applicant's chosen persona. Do not fill the result fields before the review.

### Persona 1 · Buyer understanding

**Name and role:** [Applicant to fill]  
**Review beats:** Understand the buyer; settled Understand summary.  
**Primary questions:** 1, 4 and 5.

Trust prompts:

- Which facts look confirmed, and which still need the buyer's answer?
- Can you tell where the buyer constraints came from?
- Would you send both questions as written? What makes you hesitate?

### Persona 2 · Commercial decision

**Name and role:** [Applicant to fill]  
**Review beats:** Review the offer; Compare alternatives; Why other options did not fit.  
**Primary questions:** 2, 3 and 4.

Trust prompts:

- Can you explain why this option is recommended?
- Which figures are forecasts, and which are confirmed facts?
- What would you inspect before approving it?
- Is the distinction between the AI explanation and engine calculations clear?

### Persona 3 · Buyer proposal

**Name and role:** [Applicant to fill]  
**Review beats:** Merchant preview; buyer proposal on desktop and phone.  
**Primary questions:** 2, 3 and 4 from the buyer's point of view.

Trust prompts:

- What exactly are you buying, paying and accepting?
- What is excluded?
- What happens after acceptance?
- Does anything on this page reveal the merchant's internal margins, costs or rules?

### Persona 4 · Operations and collection

**Name and role:** [Applicant to fill]  
**Review beats:** Track while waiting; review an amendment; payment rescheduled.  
**Primary questions:** 4 and 5.

Trust prompts:

- Who needs to act now: merchant, buyer or system?
- What changes in the amendment, and what stays the same?
- Is it clear that both parties approve before the payment date changes?
- Can you distinguish a scheduled payment from verified cash collected?

### Persona 5 · Product and measurement

**Name and role:** [Applicant to fill]  
**Review beats:** Full three-stage flow; Outcome and learning card; Audit details.  
**Primary questions:** all six, with emphasis on 6.

Trust prompts:

- What result will this product measure for this deal?
- Which inputs are missing from the provisional contribution figure?
- Does the product claim to learn automatically? If not, what happens before a recommendation or template changes?
- Can you verify who did what: AI, code, merchant and buyer?
