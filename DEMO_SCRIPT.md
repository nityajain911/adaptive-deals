# 90-second demo script

Start from a clean state: `npm run reset:seed`, then open `/workspace`. Click **Do this step** on the Guided demo bar to advance; it navigates to the right view each time, including the standalone buyer proposal for the two buyer steps (use **Back to merchant view** in the demo bar to return). All data is synthetic. WhatsApp is simulated and the buyer's replies are chosen by the demo operator; every simulated action is labelled on screen and in the event log.

| Time | View | Show | Say (draft — rewrite in your own voice) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–15 s | Workspace · conversation | The thread labelled **WhatsApp simulated · buyer replies selected by the demo operator**. The buyer's message with highlighted evidence beside the constraint cards: "₹50,000 available 1 Oct–31 Dec 2026", "Available from Apr 2027 (month precision)", "Above ₹60,000 needs MD approval", and the **Derived budget gap** | "A stalled deal. The AI cites the buyer's exact words; code normalises every value and works out that no budget is available from January to April." |
| 15–27 s | Workspace · conversation | Two drafts marked **Suggested by AI**, not sent. **Do this step**: the merchant sends both (**Approved and sent by merchant**); then the simulated buyer replies (**Received from buyer**); then **Use replies as confirmed answers**. The cards update and the deal moves to options ready | "The AI drafts only the two questions that change the decision. Nothing goes out until I send it." |
| 27–48 s | Decision | The **Recommended** card: Phase 1 (₹46,020), included and excluded scope, the payment schedule, **Projected contribution** ₹19,329.60 (meets the floor), **Peak funding requirement** ₹0 (within the ₹10,000 limit), within the buyer's approval limit, and **Why this structure is recommended** with its attribution line. One rejection: the 20/80 split's ₹10,500 peak, with **Rule: Cash-at-risk limit** opening **Applied merchant rules**. **Approve and send** with the exact terms hash | "Fifteen structures across four templates, all evaluated. This one works for both sides. The one beside it fails my cash-at-risk rule. Every figure comes from the engine." |
| 48–62 s | Buyer proposal (`/proposal/agr-hero/v1`, mobile-first) | The proposal link appears in the conversation only after approval. The buyer page shows scope, exclusions, the GST-inclusive total, payment dates, the recurring authorisation and what happens next, with **Agreement details** collapsed. Confirm authority and **Accept proposal**. Back in the merchant view: the hashes match, a simulated payment link, the mandate (₹23,010 limit, ends 31 Jan 2027) | "The buyer accepts the exact version I approved, on a page with none of my internal numbers. Only then is payment set up." |
| 62–82 s | Deal timeline | Mandate confirmed → Adaptive Deals' advance notice (28 Nov, for 1 Dec) → the buyer replies "Cash is tight till the 20th." → **Change requested**. **Read by code · reply-rules.v1 · not AI**: unavailable through 20 Dec, earliest debit 21 Dec → **Draft amendment** with rechecked buyer checks, projected contribution, funding requirement and mandate fit, the original 1 Dec attempt still scheduled → merchant approves v2 → buyer accepts v2 on the revised proposal → **Payment rescheduled** | "Code read the supported date phrase from the reply's own timestamp and rechecked the whole agreement. The old debit stays until both of us approve the same new terms. We deliberately kept AI out of reading payment-change replies: the phrases are narrow and an error moves money, so code handles them and everything else goes to the merchant. A broader reply reader would be its own AI task with its own evaluation." |
| 82–90 s | Deal timeline → Close | Primary metric definition (no results); the merchant approval boundary; the **No feasible deal** example (all 15 structures evaluated, none passes). Optionally, **Other outcomes** on the workspace | "We will measure realised contribution per stalled deal. When nothing works, we say so." |

The main recording does not need to show a completed upfront payment: creating the payment link and verifying its status are separate events.

**Do not say or imply that AI read the buyer's payment reply.** In the amendment scene, code read the date phrase and rechecked the agreement.

Fallbacks: if a step errors, **Reset demo** and use **Do this step** again. Every state transition is deterministic. The failed-debit path is still available under **Secondary path: restructure after a failed debit** on the timeline.
